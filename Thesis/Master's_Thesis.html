Search for Spatial Coincidence Between IceCube Neutrinos and Galactic Sources

M.Sc. Physics Dissertation

Official Thesis Page View PDF Download PDF
Abstract

Multimessenger astronomy is opening up a new field of exploration of the energetic universe through different messengers. One among these messengers is neutrinos, whose origin is still unknown. Many studies have been conducted to search for the potential sources of neutrinos using different detectors.

In this thesis, we searched for the spatial coincidence between high-energy neutrinos detected by the IceCube Neutrino Detector using the publicly available 10-year muon track data with different Galactic sources, which are gamma-ray bright red dwarfs, magnetars, and fast radio bursts. We did not find any spatial coincidence with any of the sources we analyzed, and in the case of magnetars, we also performed a stacked analysis, which similarly did not show any statistically significant excess. Our work with red dwarfs resulted in a publication in the Journal of High Energy Astrophysics, and the analysis of magnetars is currently undergoing the peer-review process.
