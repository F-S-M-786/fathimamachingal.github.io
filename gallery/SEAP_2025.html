School on Experimental Astroparticle Physics (SEAP 2025)

Cosmic Ray Laboratory, TIFR, Ooty

The School on Experimental Astroparticle Physics was held at the Cosmic Ray Laboratory, TIFR, Ooty from March 9–13, 2025. The program focused on cosmic rays, astroparticle physics, and experimental techniques.
