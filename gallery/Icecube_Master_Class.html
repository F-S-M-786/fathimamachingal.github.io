IceCube Masterclass

IceCube Neutrino Observatory Outreach Program

The IceCube Masterclass is a one-day educational program where high school students learn about astrophysics through lectures and hands-on analysis of real data from the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the South Pole.

In this program, I delivered a talk on the Moon Shadow analysis, introducing students to the observation of the cosmic-ray shadow produced by the Moon using muon data from IceCube.
