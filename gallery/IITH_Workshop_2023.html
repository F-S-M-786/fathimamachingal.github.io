Beginning Astronomy v3:Start a data-driven Journey

National Workshop on Beginner’s Astronomy (11–13 December, 2023) organized by IUCAA and the Department of Physics, IIT Hyderabad .This workshop introduced students to astronomy fundamentals and data-driven scientific methods. It included lectures, hands-on sessions, and discussions on observational astronomy.
